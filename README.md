# Gridly — AI copilot for WooCommerce (remote MCP server)

Ask Claude or ChatGPT about your WooCommerce sales, stock and orders — and make safe changes with preview and undo.

- **MCP server URL:** `https://mcp.gridlypaper.com/mcp` (Streamable HTTP, OAuth 2.1)
- **Website:** https://gridly.gridlypaper.com/?utm_source=github
- **Add in Claude:** Settings → Connectors → Browse → search "Gridly"
- **Registry name:** `com.gridlypaper/gridly`

## What you can ask
- How did my WooCommerce sales do last week compared to the week before?
- What's running low on stock right now?
- Show unpaid orders older than 3 days.
- Increase prices of all Shimano products by 8%, rounded to .99.
- Undo change 12.

## Safety
- Read-only unless you allow changes. Every change is previewed first and waits for your "yes".
- Every change gets a number; "undo change 12" restores it.
- We never store your orders, products or customers. Keys are encrypted. Disconnect anytime.
